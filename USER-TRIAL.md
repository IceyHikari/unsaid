# Classmate feedback / 同学试用记录

I asked two classmates to try different versions of Unsaid. I shared their feedback while working with Codex on 25 October 2026 and then added the trial dates. These notes paraphrase what they told me; they are not transcripts. I use P1 and P2 to keep their identities private. Device details are not included in these notes.

两位同学分别尝试了不同版本。我在 2026 年 10 月 25 日与 Codex 协作整理时补充了他们的反馈，随后补上试用日期。下面是我对意见的转述，不是逐字访谈记录；用 P1、P2 匿名区分，这份记录未包含设备信息。

## P1: 20 October 2026 / 对话版

I showed P1 the first playable conversation on the day I received it, 20 October. This corresponds to the version preserved in commit `8f5634f`.

P1 found it too simple and passive, similar to following a branching visual novel. Most of the activity was choosing a reply and reading the next part of the story.

I agreed that the player needed more to do in the scene. I asked for direct manipulation of a studio instead of adding further dialogue branches. The revision on 21 October introduced the movable lamp, inspectable objects and painting placement. I kept undo and bilingual presentation.

10 月 20 日收到首个可玩对话版后，我当天就给 P1 看了。他觉得体验比较单调，像跟着分支剧情往下走，主要是在选回应和读下一段。我也认为玩家需要更多参与，因此要求把操作放进工作室场景里。10 月 21 日版本加入了灯光、物件查看与画作位置选择，同时保留撤回和双语。

## P2: Thursday 22 October 2026 / 场景版

P2 liked the light design, the photograph and study textures, and the Chinese/English options. Their concerns were more specific to how the room behaved:

- When the painting left the wall, the light stopped affecting it. They were disappointed that an interaction they enjoyed disappeared on the rack and in the packing area.
- They did not understand the label's purpose and thought its appearance needed improvement.
- “Open the doors” did not feel like a clear ending. Returning to the same room seemed to be the main next action, and they wanted different outcomes to feel more distinct.

I kept the photograph, studies and language switching. On 25 October, I requested lighting at every painting destination, a clearer label with an explicit displayed/set-aside state, and illustrated morning scenes based on the final arrangement. Each ending now has its own closing text, with separate options to revise the exhibition or begin again.

P2 在 10 月 22 日星期四尝试了场景版。他喜欢灯光、照片和草稿的质感，也觉得双语做得不错。但画离开墙面后灯光就不起作用了，他觉得失望；展签的意义和外观不清楚；开门之后又回到房间，也让他不太明白结局是什么。

我保留他认可的部分，在 25 日要求补齐三个位置的灯光响应、展签的公开状态，以及与布置对应的不同清晨场景。结局现在有各自的收尾文字，并明确区分重新布置和从头体验。

## How the trials started / 试用顺序

On 26 October, I confirmed that a classmate had tried the experience independently before I explained the controls. I have not recorded which participant that confirmation refers to, so I do not describe it as a separate observation for both sessions. The later automatic introductory guide belongs to the revised design; it was not part of these earlier versions.

10 月 26 日，我确认了同学在我讲解操作之前已经先自行尝试。这个确认没有记录具体对应 P1 还是 P2，所以没有分别写成两次观察。现在自动弹出的引导是后续修改，不能倒写成早期试用时已有的功能。

## After the feedback / 修改之后

The feedback-led changes passed developer browser checks. I have not recorded a classmate retest of the latest version. The packing and introductory-guide changes on 25 October came from my own subsequent review, not an additional classmate session.

反馈对应的修改已通过浏览器技术检查，最新版还没有记录同学复测。25 日后续对包装和首次引导的调整来自我自己的检查，没有写成新的同学试用。

If I run another trial, I will let the person start without explaining the controls, note their first actions and hesitation, and then ask what changed because of their actions. I would record the date, device, language, help given and resulting design decision. I have kept those as follow-up steps rather than filled-in observations.

如果再做一轮，我会先让对方自行尝试，记录第一步操作、犹豫点和对反馈的理解，再问他觉得自己的操作改变了什么。同时补记日期、设备、语言、我是否提供帮助，以及据此作出的修改决定。这些是后续步骤，不是已经完成的观察。
