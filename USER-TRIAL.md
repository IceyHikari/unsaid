# User trial guide / 真人试用步骤

This is a blank protocol, not a completed trial. / 这是试用步骤，尚未完成真人试用。

1. Ask one person willing to try a short fictional conversation. Do not explain the controls or intended message beforehand. Let them choose English or Chinese.
2. Say only: “Please try this experience. Tell me what you are thinking as you use it.”
3. Observe their first action, hesitation, understanding of the character's response, and attempts to go back or restart. Allow them to finish independently; record help if you give it.
4. Afterwards ask: “What changed because of your choices?”, “Was anything unclear?”, and “What would you try next?”
5. Record date, device, language, anonymous participant label, observed behavior and feedback. Record one design change or a specific reason to retain the current behavior in PROCESS.md. Retest any change.

请找一位愿意体验短篇虚构对话的人，先不解释玩法或主题。只说：“请试试这个作品，操作时可以说说你在想什么。”观察第一步操作、犹豫点、是否理解反馈，以及是否会回退或重启。结束后询问：“你的选择改变了什么？”“哪里不清楚？”“接下来想试什么？”将真实观察发给作者与开发助手，再决定改动。不需要公开姓名或录音录像。
