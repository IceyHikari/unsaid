## AI assistance / AI 协作

I used Codex to interpret the brief, propose the initial scene, draft bilingual dialogue and write substantial parts of the HTML/CSS/JavaScript, inline SVG artwork, documentation and browser verification scripts. I asked it to revise the passive conversation format into an interactive room and specified changes to lighting, packing, endings, onboarding and the English-first entry. Codex also helped rewrite these notes in my first-person voice.

The fictional bereavement scene was proposed and drafted with AI assistance. It is not an autobiographical account. No AI model or external generation service runs inside the finished experience.

我使用 Codex 协助梳理要求、提出初始情境、起草双语对白，并编写了大量程序、内联 SVG 素材、文档和浏览器验证脚本。我要求它从被动对话改为画面交互，并提出灯光、包装、结局、引导与英文入口的具体修改。它也协助把文档整理成我的第一人称叙述。虚构的丧亲情节由 AI 协助提出和起草，不是我的亲历；作品运行时不调用 AI 服务。
